<workflow name="traction-research">

<objective>
Research what technical content about AI development tools, Claude Code, and agentic coding is currently performing well across platforms. Identify formats, hooks, angles, and distribution patterns that are driving engagement. Produce an actionable report to inform future content strategy.
</objective>

<process>

1. **Platform-specific research**:

   **X/Twitter Developer Community:**
   - Search for high-engagement tweets/threads about Claude Code, AI coding, agentic development
   - Identify thread structures that get retweets (hook patterns, thread length, code inclusion)
   - Note which developer accounts have strong engagement in this niche
   - Track trending hashtags and conversation topics

   **LinkedIn Engineering Content:**
   - Search for AI/developer tools posts with high engagement
   - Identify what framing works (lessons learned, contrarian takes, how-to, results)
   - Note optimal post length and structure patterns
   - Track which professional angles resonate (productivity, team impact, career)

   **Dev.to / HackerNews / Technical Blogs:**
   - Search for recent high-performing posts about AI coding tools
   - Identify title patterns that drive clicks
   - Analyze comment sections for what readers respond to most
   - Note content gaps — what topics are underserved

   **Newsletter Landscape:**
   - Identify developer newsletters covering AI tools
   - Note subject line patterns with high open rates
   - Track what exclusive content drives subscriptions

2. **Pattern synthesis**:
   - Top 5 performing content formats right now
   - Top 5 hooks/angles getting engagement
   - Content gaps and underserved topics
   - Audience sentiment (what are developers excited/frustrated about?)
   - Optimal publishing timing and frequency

3. **Actionable recommendations**:
   - Next 3 blog post topics based on traction patterns + your session history
   - Recommended angles for each platform
   - Hooks to test
   - Distribution strategy (when and where to post)

Save to `outputs/devlog-publisher/{date}/traction_report.md`.

</process>

<success_criteria>
- Research covers at least 3 platforms
- At least 5 high-performing content examples analyzed
- Clear pattern identification with specific examples
- Actionable recommendations tied to the user's actual session insights
- Content gap analysis identifies underserved topics
</success_criteria>

</workflow>
