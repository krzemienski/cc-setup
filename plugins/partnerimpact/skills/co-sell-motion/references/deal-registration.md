# Deal Registration

Deal registration protects a partner's investment in sourcing an opportunity. Register a deal, get a
window of protection and a margin/support benefit. Done well, it rewards partners for bringing you
business. Done badly, it becomes a land-grab where partners register deals they didn't source to
claim protection they didn't earn.

## What registration is for

- **Protect the sourcing partner** from being undercut by direct or another partner on a deal they
  brought.
- **Create a clean attribution trail**: registration is the primary evidence of partner sourcing.
- **Incentivize early disclosure**: partners tell you about deals sooner because registering early
  secures the protection.

## What registration must capture

A registration is only as good as the evidence behind it. Require:

- **Account and opportunity**: company, buying center, use case.
- **Evidence of sourcing**: how the partner originated or is materially advancing it. A named
  contact, a specific event, an intro made. Not just "we know them."
- **Deal specifics**: expected size, timeline, products.
- **The partner's role**: sourcing only, or co-selling through to close.

## The conflict decision tree

When two claims collide, resolve in this order:

1. **Is there a prior valid registration?** First valid, evidenced registration wins. "Valid" =
   real sourcing evidence, not a speculative land-grab. This is the default and it should resolve
   most cases.
2. **If direct was already working the account** (documented in CRM before the registration): the
   deal is direct; the partner may still earn an influence credit if they advance it.
3. **If two partners both have a claim**: precedence to the earlier valid registration; if
   simultaneous, weigh evidence of origination, then customer preference.
4. **If evidence is thin on all sides**: no protection granted; treat as direct until someone
   demonstrates sourcing. Don't reward the loudest claim.

The tree only works if "valid registration" has teeth. A registration with no sourcing evidence is
not valid, no matter how early it was filed.

## The protection you grant

- **A time window**: e.g. 60–90 days, renewable on evidence of active progression. Expired,
  unworked registrations release the account.
- **A margin or support benefit**: the registered partner gets protected pricing, deal support, or
  a registration discount.
- **Exclusivity on that opportunity**: for the window, direct and other partners stand down.

Calibrate the window and benefit to your motion. Too generous and partners register everything; too
stingy and they don't bother.

## Registration mistakes

- **No sourcing evidence required**: turns registration into a claim-staking game.
- **Rewarding registration over outcome**: a partner who registers 50 deals and closes none is
  gaming, not selling. Track registration-to-close, not just registration volume.
- **Windows that never expire**: dead registrations freeze accounts. Expire and release them.
- **No CRM check for prior direct activity**: grants protection on deals direct already owned,
  creating exactly the conflict registration was meant to prevent.

## Output

1. The registration fields required (with sourcing evidence mandatory).
2. The conflict decision tree, adapted to the user's motion.
3. The protection granted (window, benefit, exclusivity).
4. The metric to watch: registration-to-close rate, not registration volume.
