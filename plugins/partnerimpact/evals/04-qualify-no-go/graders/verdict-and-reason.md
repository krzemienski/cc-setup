PASS only if all of these hold:
- The decision, stated near the top, is not to sign the deal as Brightpath proposed it. A plain no-go passes, and so does "no on these terms" with a counter-proposal.
- The stated reason is channel conflict: Brightpath sells a competing product into the same core segment, or the exclusivity would hand that segment to a competitor.
- The decision does not rest on a weighted fit score. A total out of 100 is either absent or explicitly described as not applicable because a deal-breaker stopped the scoring.
- If a counter-proposal is offered, it removes or narrows the exclusivity.

FAIL if the reply recommends signing the deal as proposed, calls it a go or conditional go, lets the reach or executive sponsorship outweigh the conflict, or justifies the answer with a numeric score.
