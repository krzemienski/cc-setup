PASS only if all of these hold:
- The decision is go (or "sign them"), stated near the top. It is not labelled conditional go or "not yet". Naming contract points to settle or a review date alongside the go is fine.
- The weighted score is 84 out of 100, or the band is given as Priority.
- All five deal-breaker checks are shown as cleared, each tied to a fact from the brief.
- The ratings are attributed to the user, and the weights are identified as the standard or default ones.

FAIL if the decision is labelled conditional go or "not yet", if the score differs, or if a deal-breaker is marked open although the brief answers it.
